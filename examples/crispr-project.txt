CRISPR-Cas9 delivery project

This example is for demonstrating Again. It does not describe a real person or a verified study.

Project context
I worked on a supervised student project comparing CRISPR-Cas9 delivery conditions in mammalian cell culture.

My contribution
I prepared guide RNA controls, performed DNA extraction and PCR, and tracked cell passage number and reagent timing in a lab notebook.

What changed
When results were inconsistent, I compared the recorded conditions across experiments. I revised the preparation checklist so each experiment used the same passage range and documented reagent timing.

What I would discuss next
I would like to understand how your lab documents controls, reviews unexpected results, and maintains reproducible protocols as the team grows.
