# Trust and encounter context

Staylinked addresses a narrow part of **Side A: Trust coming in, the applicant flood**: preserving the context of an encounter so a recruiter can recover why a person was memorable when reviewing their work later. It does not authenticate applicants, verify qualifications, or process an entire application queue.

## Current mechanisms

| Mechanism                                          | How it helps                                                             | Limit                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Event invitation shared by QR or link              | Associates a connection with an event and recruiter                      | The invitation can be forwarded. Access does not prove attendance, identity, or a personal meeting |
| Candidate-authored recap and memorable detail      | Gives the recruiter a record without requiring them to write every recap | The candidate must complete it, and the account is not independently verified                      |
| Original recap retained alongside edits            | Makes the first submission available when context changes                | Preservation establishes what was submitted, not whether it was true                               |
| Editable profile, project notes, and uploaded work | Keeps current material attached to the existing connection               | The work is author supplied; authorship and factual claims are not verified                        |
| Exact source passages beside a selected role       | Lets the recruiter inspect the supplied text relevant to a requirement   | Word matching can miss synonyms or surface negated claims. It does not establish competence        |

Connections have no public discovery route. The recruiter chooses whether to contact someone through their existing links. The application assigns no candidate score and sends no automated outreach.

## What LinkedIn already supports

LinkedIn's QR feature already connects offline encounters to online profiles: scanning opens the person's profile so the member can connect. QR exchange is an existing interaction. [LinkedIn QR documentation](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a525286/using-a-linkedin-qr-code-to-connect-with-members?lang=en)

LinkedIn provides verification for identity, workplace, and educational affiliation. Its job badges provide information about the company and job poster; LinkedIn explicitly distinguishes this from job suitability. These are authenticity signals with a different scope from retaining a conversation. [Profile verification](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1355912/), [job verification](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1497886)

Recruiter supports notes, visibility controls, and links to candidate websites or portfolios. The documented note workflow asks the recruiter to compose the note. It also provides projects, talent pools, and pipelines, with a rediscovery feature that can surface candidates from previous recruiting activity for eligible customers. Staylinked does not introduce these categories of record keeping or rediscovery. [Profile notes](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a414117), [profile resources](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a416553), [projects](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a412509/), [candidate rediscovery](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7129158)

The differentiation is an **unmeasured workflow hypothesis**: making the candidate's event recap the starting record, then placing maintained work and role-relevant passages beside it, may reduce the effort needed to reconstruct a meeting. This combines existing interactions around one task. The research above does not establish that LinkedIn cannot support a similar workflow or that Staylinked is faster.

## Evaluation

- **Recall accuracy:** after a delay, ask recruiters to identify the correct person, event, and discussed detail. Record correct answers and mistaken associations.
- **Time to supporting material:** give recruiters a role requirement and measure time to locate the relevant original passage, recording incorrect or unsupported selections.
- **Recap-writing burden:** count recruiter-authored words, corrections, and time needed to obtain a usable encounter record. Also record candidate completion and effort so moving the work does not get mistaken for eliminating it.

No results for these measures have been collected.
